Features

Features.

01

Daily puzzle

A new puzzle opens every midnight. Yesterday's board closes; only today's remains.

02

Multiple difficulties

From a quick round to one you'll sit with, pick the difficulty that fits.

03

Notes and hints

Pencil marks, hints, undo, and pause — the minimum set of tools that won't disrupt your solve.

04

Live score and timer

Check your running score and elapsed time right on the board.

05

Completion stats

Finished boards accumulate into records, broken down by difficulty.

06

Remove ads

Every board is free to solve. Removing ads is a one-time in-app purchase.
