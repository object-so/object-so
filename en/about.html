Vision & Philosophy

Apps today drift to the extremes — super-apps that try to be everything, or hyper-casual apps you forget the next day.

Object stakes a place in between. One clear purpose. A tool built precisely for that purpose. Not more features, but a better single one. We don't chase trends; we make apps that stay useful long after the trend is gone.

Values

Focus

We commit to one thing, deeply. The pull to add more is constant — but we don't ship anything that blurs the original purpose.

Craft

Details and finish are how trust is built. We polish what no one will see, then ship.

Simplicity

Only what's needed, clearly. The first time someone opens our app, they shouldn't have to think about what to do.
