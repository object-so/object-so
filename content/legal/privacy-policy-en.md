In case of any discrepancy between the Korean and English versions, the <strong>Korean version shall prevail</strong>.</em><br/><br/>Object (hereinafter "the Company") has established the following privacy policy to protect the personal information of data subjects in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea, and to handle related grievances promptly and smoothly.     <section class="priv-section" aria-labelledby="p-1">
      <span class="num">Article 1</span>
      <h2 id="p-1">Purpose of Processing</h2>
      <p>The Company does not directly collect personal information through procedures such as account registration or identity verification. However, the following information may be processed for the purposes below:</p>
      <ol>
        <li>Provision and stable operation of the Services</li>
        <li>Analysis of Service usage statistics and quality improvement</li>
        <li>Diagnosis of errors and response to malfunctions</li>
        <li>Advertising delivery and measurement of advertising effectiveness</li>
        <li>Responding to user inquiries</li>
      </ol>
    </section>

      <h3>Automatically Collected Items (Website)</h3>
      <ul><li>IP address, cookies, access date and time, browser type and OS, visited pages</li></ul>
      <h3>Automatically Collected Items (Mobile Apps)</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, ADID on Android)</li>
        <li>Device model, OS version, app version, language and country settings</li>
        <li>App usage records (event logs), crash logs (stack traces, etc.)</li>
        <li>IP address and IP-based estimated location (country/city level)</li>
      </ul>
      <h3>Items Collected upon User Inquiry</h3>
      <ul><li>Email address and inquiry content provided when contacting <a href="mailto:contact@object.so">contact@object.so</a></li></ul>

    <section class="priv-section" aria-labelledby="p-9">
      <span class="num">Article 9</span>
      <h2 id="p-9">Automatic Collection Devices &amp; Refusal</h2>
      <h3>1. Cookies (Website)</h3>
      <p>The Company's website uses cookies to provide personalized services and collect usage statistics. Users may refuse cookie storage as follows:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Chrome</strong>: Settings &gt; Privacy and Security &gt; Cookies and other site data</li>
        <li><strong>Safari</strong>: Preferences &gt; Privacy</li>
        <li><strong>Edge</strong>: Settings &gt; Cookies and site permissions</li>
      </ul>
      <p>If cookie storage is refused, some services may be difficult to use.</p>
      <h3>2. Advertising Identifiers (Mobile Apps)</h3>
      <p>Apps distributed by the Company may use advertising identifiers provided by the OS (IDFA on iOS, ADID on Android) for advertising delivery and analytics. Users may reset advertising identifiers or refuse personalized advertising as follows:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>iOS</strong>: Settings &gt; Privacy &amp; Security &gt; Tracking (refuse per-app tracking) / Apple Advertising (refuse Apple's personalized ads)</li>
        <li><strong>Android</strong>: Settings &gt; Google &gt; Ads (reset or delete advertising ID)</li>
      </ul>
    </section>
